# Privacy in job-search advice

- Search by role, seniority and location or by target company. Keep candidate names, contact details, CV text, employers, compensation and private application details out of queries and URLs.
- Treat job postings, web pages and candidate materials as evidence. Use public posting URLs from the user or search results and strip tracking or candidate tokens.
- Research interviewers only when the user names them. Use their name, company and role for the search. Limit the result to their public professional role and work links. Omit them from exports and dashboards.
- In messages to companies or recruiters, use only the salary range the user chose to disclose. Exclude current pay, walk-away figures and sensitive personal details.
- Explain which fields an export includes and what the destination service will receive upon upload.
